Quick glossary

PO match
A purchase order whose cargo, route and ready date fit a sailing that still has space.
TEU / 20ft / 40ft
Container slots we hold on each vessel. A 40ft uses two TEU.
Gate-in cut-off
Last time a loaded container can enter the port for that sailing.
SI & VGM cut-off
Deadline for shipping instructions and the verified gross mass of each container.
LEO
Let Export Order, customs clearance for the shipping bill.
